---
name: use-journal
description: "Keep a private record of personal highlights, cross-references and notes beside the Bible text."
---

Open the requested passage with open_reader. Preserve the user’s words when adding a note, highlight or verse link. Use list_journal to identify an existing entry. A create_share_link call makes one selected item readable by anyone with its link; make it only when that sharing is requested. Use revoke_share_link to withdraw access.
