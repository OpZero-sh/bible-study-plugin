---
name: use-lexicon
description: "Explore a biblical Greek or Hebrew word by spelling, English gloss or Strong’s number."
---

Use a G- or H-prefixed number with get_strongs_entry; search_original_word accepts a spelling or transliteration. Use search_by_meaning as a discovery step, then identify candidate entries by number. An English gloss alone does not prove which original word appears in a particular verse.
